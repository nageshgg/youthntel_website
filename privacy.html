Website Privacy

This notice covers visits to the YouthIntel informational website.

Draft for operator review. Hosting arrangements, the responsible operator, the privacy contact and any handling of correspondence are awaiting confirmation.

Information on this website

These pages are served as static files. The website has no accounts, forms, analytics or advertising trackers. Its own code does not set cookies or store browsing information on your device.

The website is intended for general information. Please avoid sharing information about identifiable children, patient records, health details or safeguarding cases.

Hosting and technical logs

GitHub Pages is the intended host. Hosting and network providers may receive technical information when they serve a page, such as IP addresses, requested URLs and browser information. Details of GitHub’s handling of personal information are available in its privacy statement.

Correspondence and privacy enquiries

The contact section lists our general enquiries email. The email link opens your email application. When you send a message, the recipient receives your email address and message content.
